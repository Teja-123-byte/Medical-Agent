# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its contributors.
- Protect privacy and never share real patient data or secrets.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the contact method configured for the repository. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Security concerns should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports fairly and take appropriate action, including warnings, content removal, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from project participation.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual is representing the project or its community.
